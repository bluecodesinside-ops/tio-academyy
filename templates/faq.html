Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses

How do I get started with the courses?

Simply browse our course catalog and choose the dance style that interests you. Each course is designed for beginners and progresses to advanced levels.

Do I need any special equipment?

No special equipment is required. Just wear comfortable clothing and shoes that allow you to move freely. A dance floor or open space is recommended.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Absolutely! All our courses are self-paced, so you can learn whenever it's convenient for you. You have lifetime access to the course materials.
